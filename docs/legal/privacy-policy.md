## 1. What we collect, and why

| Data                                                          | Why                                                                                      | Legal basis                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Name, college email, roll number, branch, batch               | To verify you belong to the college and run your account                                 | Consent at sign-up; legitimate use for the college's alumni programme |
| Profile details you add (headline, company, skills, phone)    | To let members find mentors and alumni, only as visible as you choose                    | Your consent; each field has a visibility setting                     |
| Posts, questions, answers, messages, requests, RSVPs          | To provide the features you use                                                          | Your consent                                                          |
| Roadmap progress, points, badges                              | To show your progress; leaderboards only if you opt in                                   | Your consent                                                          |
| Sign-in records (device type, approximate network /24, times) | Security and abuse prevention                                                            | Legitimate use (security)                                             |
| Optional: college ID photo                                    | Manual verification when the roll-number check fails; deleted 30 days after the decision | Your consent                                                          |

We **do not** sell or share personal data, run advertising, or use third-party trackers. Analytics shown to the college are aggregated, and groups smaller than five people are hidden.

## 2. Who can see your data

- **Other members:** only the profile fields you allow (contact details are hidden by default).
- **Messages:** only you and the other person. College staff and the platform operator cannot read them; moderators see only a message that was reported to them.
- **College staff:** what their role needs (for example, verification reviewers see your verification request).
- **Service providers** under contract (hosting in India, email delivery), only to run the service.

## 3. How long we keep it

See the retention table in the college's settings and `docs/privacy.md`. Highlights: verification codes 24 hours, ID photos 30 days after the decision, stories 24 hours, closed job listings 60 days, résumés on referral requests 60 days, deleted accounts erased after a grace period ([30] days).

## 4. Your rights (DPDP Act 2023)

- **Access and export:** Settings → Your data → Download my data.
- **Correction:** edit your profile at any time.
- **Withdraw consent:** Settings → Privacy (optional purposes).
- **Erasure:** Settings → Your data → Delete my account. You can cancel during the grace period.
- **Grievance:** contact the Grievance Officer above. If unresolved, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
- **Nominate:** [process for nominating another person to exercise your rights].
