# U3 · Career paths (roadmaps, rebuilt)

Prompt 3, Part U, phase U3. Built on U1's primitives and U2's spaces (Career paths is the first section of Learn). Shipped in two parts: **U3a**, the student side (this document's first half), and **U3b**, the map, authoring and review.

## Who it is for and the job

A second-year who is not sure what to become: pick a direction in under a minute, see exactly what to do this week, and feel progress every time they open it. Then the alumni who write paths and the faculty who review them.

## Problems it fixes (§3)

| Problem                                                                                 | What U3 changes                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The Galaxy: a canvas of small labels, dark only, hard to read and slow on a phone       | Removed from the member UI. The index is a list: what you follow first, then paths for your branch, then every path by category |
| A path was a graph or a flat list of 10–40 steps; nobody could say what to do this week | The journey: the steps in 3–6 phases of about equal time, the next step marked, finished phases folded away                     |
| "2 steps a week" said nothing about time                                                | A pace in hours a week ("Your pace: 6 h a week · Change"), and every time shown at it ("about 1–2 weeks")                       |
| Sources, branches, review dates and a disclaimer above the steps                        | One line under the title (what it leads to), one action (Start or Continue); the rest in "About this"                           |
| Marking a step done had no way back                                                     | Mark done shows an Undo toast; the celebration is kept                                                                          |

- **Continue**: each followed path as an `ActionCard`: the path's name as the kicker, the next step as the title, "Step 3 of 10 · about 2 weeks at your pace" (or "at 6 h a week" without a pace), a progress ring, and Continue (opens the path with that step's sheet). A finished path says "All 10 steps done" with Look back.
- **For your branch**: published paths whose branches include the member's (from their profile), not followed yet; a sideways-scrolling row on phones, a grid from tablet width. Each card: the title (wraps, never cut), "For roles like …" from `outcomes[0]`, and its size at the stated pace.
- **All paths**: by category in `ListSection`s; search inline; category chips and the branch in the `Toolbar` (a Filters sheet on phones). Authors see their drafts; reviewers reach the queue from the overflow.

## Detail: the journey (`roadmap-page.tsx`, `path-header.tsx`, `journey.tsx`, `step-sheet.tsx`)

```
360 ─────────────────────────────────
 ‹ Career paths
 Backend Developer               ⓘ ⋯
 For roles like Backend Developer
 [ ▶ Start this path ]
 10 steps · about 20 weeks at 6 h a week
                         [Steps | Map]
 Foundations                 3 steps
 ○ One server-side language    ⌘
   Next · about 2–4 weeks
 ○ Git and the command line
   about 4–8 h
 Core skills                 3 steps
 …
```

- **Header**: title, "For roles like …", one primary action (Start this path, or Continue to the next step). Following: a progress bar, "3 of 10 steps · next: Arrays and hashing", and the pace line. Overflow: history (review changes for reviewers), edit, stop following. "About this": the summary, every outcome, category, branches, author or library, last reviewed, version, the path's sources, the disclaimer. Reporting stays in its own menu.
- **Phases** (`journeyPhases()` in `packages/shared`, unit-tested): steps sorted by depth in the prerequisite graph, then cut into 3–6 phases (one per four steps, at least three) at the points closest to equal time, preferring cuts between depths. Names by position: Foundations, Core skills, Go deeper, Build and prove, Practise, Get interview-ready (Get exam-ready for exam paths). A step never lands in an earlier phase than its prerequisites.
- **Steps** (`StepTrack`): done, next or upcoming (nothing is locked: a student may open any step), the title (wraps), the time at the member's pace, the kind as an icon, "Proof" on projects.
- **Step sheet** (`?step=` in the URL; a bottom sheet on phones, a side panel on desktop): kind and time; "What and why"; "How you'll know you're done" (the proof, one item per line or clause); resources and sources as link rows (site, opens in a new tab); comments for authors and reviewers; the footer: Mark done (celebration and Undo) or Mark as not done, and Next step.
- **Pace**: Start follows without a goal (no nudges until the member chooses one). The goal is now hours a week (0–40) instead of steps a week; a goal saved the old way reads as the hours those steps take on average in that path. The weekly nudge compares the likely hours of the steps done in the last seven days with the pace.
- **Map**: the existing graph, loaded only when chosen (U3b rebuilds it).

## States

- Loading: skeletons at the final sizes (header, button, rows).
- Empty: no path matches → "No roadmaps match", try another category or search. No branch on the profile → no "For your branch" section.
- Offline: marking a step done waits in the outbox (B13) and shows as done meanwhile.
- Unverified or a minor: the same screens; Start and Mark done are shown only with `roadmaps:progress`.
- Not published (an author's draft): the draft banner; no Start.

Career paths · Continue · For {branch} · All paths · Start this path · Mark done · Next step · "“{step}” done" with Undo · Your pace: 6 h a week · Change · Set your pace · On track this week · This week: 2 of 6 h · Show 2 finished phases (8 steps) · What and why · How you'll know you're done · For roles like {role}.

## U3b

- **Map**: fitted to the screen on open, the next step in the middle, titles that wrap (three lines), pan by dragging or scrolling, zoom by pinch, Ctrl + wheel, + and −, or the buttons (0 fits again), a small key.
- **Editor**: an outline in the author's order (the phase each step lands in, "no source yet"), drag or arrows to reorder, problems on the step they name, and "What students will see", the journey drawn from the draft.
- **Review**: a side-by-side diff of the version against the one before it, every changed value old and new (`del` and `ins`), stacked on a phone.
- **Moved**: "Seniors on this path" and "Ask a senior" to U6; "Drives that ask for these skills" and "Practise this" to U7, where their data is (nothing is shown until it is real).

## Acceptance

- Picking a path and starting it: Learn → a path → Start this path = 2 taps (≤ 3).
- Mark done on the next step: Home's next-step card → Mark done = 2 taps (≤ 2).
- No title cut at 360 in te or hi; zero axe violations on the index, the journey and the step sheet; e2e for start, mark done and undo.
- LCP ≤ 2.5 s on the index and a path (measured in U13 with the rest).
