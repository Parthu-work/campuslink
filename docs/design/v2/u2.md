# U2 · Information architecture and navigation

Prompt 3, Part U, phase U2. Built on U1's primitives. The model lives in `packages/shared/src/navigation.ts` only (`buildNav()`); the web app renders it.

## Who it is for and the job

Every member, every screen: find anything in two taps and always know where you are. A student between classes wants Learn, Mentors or Career at a thumb's reach; a TPO wants the day's queue first and every admin section one level down, not in a second column.

Landing tab: final-year students (the last two years) land on Career; first-years on Learn; everyone else on the first space. - Phone: the space's landing screen lists its sections (tabs on a `TabNav` row, or a `ListSection` where they are many: You, Students). Create leaves the bottom bar: it is each space's primary action, and a floating button on Home only.
- One inbox entry in the top bar: messages and notifications share one count; the entry opens Notifications with a segmented switch to Messages (two screens, as today).
- Every non-root screen: `BackLink` and a compact title; breadcrumbs (space › section) on desktop only.
- Search (the command palette, lazily loaded) covers people, companies, drives, roadmaps, subjects and actions, with recent items.

## States

- Loading: the shell paints from `buildNav()` with no data; counts fill in later (no layout shift: the count's space is reserved only when non-zero, in the trailing slot).
- Offline: the shell and every cached section stay reachable; a section without cached data shows its offline state.
- Minor: same spaces; sections minors cannot open are hidden (permissions already exclude them).
- Read-only (non-payment): unchanged; spaces stay, actions explain.

- Unit tests per persona: the spaces, each destination in exactly one space, permissions and feature switches removing sections and empty spaces, minors, the landing space for first- and final-years.
- No persona's desktop sidebar has more than 7 top-level entries (test).
- e2e: ten deep links (a roadmap, a drive, a company page, readiness, a circle, a message thread, admin › onboarding, a session, a job, settings) each light the owning tab on a phone and the owning space on desktop.
- Labels fit untruncated at 360 in en, te and hi (the capture audit checks the bottom bar).
- Tap counts: every U0.2 task stays equal or improves.
