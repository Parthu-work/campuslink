# U5 · Home: one clear "Today"

Prompt 3, Part U, phase U5. Built on U1's primitives (`ActionCard`, `ListRow`, `PersonCard`, `Countdown`), U2's spaces and U4's set-up checklist.

## Who it is for and the job

A student opening the app between classes: in two seconds, the one thing that needs them now (a session about to start, a drive about to close, or this week's step), then what else is worth doing, then what their college is saying. An alumnus: who is waiting for them. Staff get U9's Today.

## Problems it fixes (§3)

| Problem                                                                                    | What U5 changes                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Home stacked equal-weight cards: next session, Next Step, One Useful Thing, composer, feed | One large "Today" card for the single most urgent thing; everything else is a list or the feed                      |
| "One useful thing today" wrapped a post in a second, featured card style                   | Gone: the feed is "From your college", one post card style                                                          |
| A stories row with one or two rings                                                        | Shown only with at least three active stories; "Add a story" stays one tap away for members who post                |
| "Suggested for you" was a list of names with a Follow button each at full weight           | One row of `PersonCard`s with the real reason ("Alumnus of your branch, CSE"), Follow at secondary weight           |
| The desktop column had three promotional cards (find a mentor, Q&A, impact)                | A narrow "Coming up": sessions, deadlines and events                                                                |
| An alumnus's Home looked like a student's                                                  | Leads with "Waiting for you": mentorship requests (Accept in one tap) and micro-asks (Accept or Decline in one tap) |

## The Today card (`features/home/today.tsx`)

One `ActionCard`, the first that applies:

1. **A session within 15 minutes, or happening now** (`/bookings?when=upcoming`): kicker "Starts in 12 min" or "Happening now" (`Countdown`), the session and the other person, its time, and **Join**, which opens Sessions with that session's join dialog (`/sessions?join=<id>`).
2. **A drive closing within 48 hours** that the student is eligible for, can still register for and has not (`/placements/drives?scope=eligible`): kicker "Registration closes" with a countdown, the company and drive, and **Register** (the drive page).
3. **This week's Next Step** (G7): its path step ("Next on Software Engineer"), **Continue** to the step (Mark done is the next tap, U3). Without a path step, its first other item.

Nothing applies: no card. Home's route loader fetches the sessions, the drives and the Next Step with the page, so the card paints with Home and nothing moves.

## The rest

- **Your next steps**: the Next Step's other items as `ListRow`s (at most three, titles wrap), without the one already in the Today card; "See all" opens `/next-step`.
- **Suggested for you**: a sideways row of `PersonCard`s from the existing suggestions (alumni of the student's branch first). The reason is what the suggestion is based on: "Alumnus of your branch (CSE)" or "Alumnus of your college"; nothing invented (X7). One Follow per card, secondary weight.
- **From your college**: a heading, then the For you / Following feed (the day's One Useful Thing is simply a post in it) and the composer for those who post. "Add a story" sits by the heading when the stories row is hidden.
- **Stories**: the row shows only with three or more active stories.
- **Coming up** (wide screens, a narrow right column): up to three upcoming sessions, drives closing in the next 7 days the student can still register for, and up to three upcoming events, each one row with its time. Phones leave it out: the Today card carries what is urgent.
- **Alumni**: "Waiting for you" after the Today card: pending mentorship requests (the student, what they asked for; **Accept** in one tap, **Decline…** on the Requests screen, where the reason is chosen) and micro-asks waiting for their reply (**Accept** or **Decline**, one tap each). Nothing waiting: the section is left out.

## Acceptance

Home's LCP ≤ 2.5 s (`audit:lighthouse` home-cold; measured 6.0 s against 6.2 s before U5 on the same machine, so the load path moves to U10); CLS ≤ 0.05 (the Today card paints with the page); zero axe violations in both themes; en/te/hi at 360 with no overflow; e2e: a student with a session in the next 15 minutes sees Join on Home; the Next Step card when nothing is urgent; an alumnus accepts a request from Home in one tap.
