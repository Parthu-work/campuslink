# U9 · Staff: Today, tables and the TPO console

Prompt 3, Part U, phase U9. Built on U1's primitives (`ListRow`, `ListSection`, `Countdown`, `Table`, `Toolbar`, `Sheet`) and U2's staff spaces (Today, Students, Placements, Content, College). Shipped in three parts: **U9a**, Today (the staff home); **U9b**, one table pattern for staff lists; **U9c**, dashboards that lead with four numbers and their change.

## Who it is for and the job

A placement officer (TPO), a faculty member or a college admin opening the app between classes: see what is waiting, oldest and most urgent first, and act on it in one tap. When nothing is waiting, know that, and what is next.

## Problems it fixes (§3)

| Problem                                                               | What U9 changes                                                                          |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A19: tiles whose titles wrap one word per line ("Not / joined / yet") | Rows: the work in a sentence that wraps as a sentence ("12 students haven't joined yet") |
| A19: a "Waiting" badge colliding with a truncated title ("Modera…")   | No badge: a row exists only when something is waiting                                    |
| A19: zero-count tiles at the same weight as urgent ones               | Items at zero are hidden; the order is by urgency (drives closing first)                 |
| The overview repeated every admin section under the tiles             | Gone: the staff spaces list their sections (U2)                                          |
| Members and activity shown as if they were work                       | One quiet line, "This week", linking to analytics                                        |

## U9a · Today (`features/admin/today.tsx`, `/admin`)

```
360 ──────────────────────────────
 Today
 ┌──────────────────────────────┐
 │ 1 drive closes in 2 days     │
 │ Nimbus Labs, in 5 hours      │
 │                     [Open]   │
 ├──────────────────────────────┤
 │ 3 offers to verify           │
 │ Oldest waiting 2 days        │
 │                    [Verify]  │
 ├──────────────────────────────┤
 │ 12 students haven't joined   │
 │                    [Invite]  │
 └──────────────────────────────┘
 This week: 1,204 members · 312 active   ›

All clear ────────────────────────
 Nothing waiting.
 Next drive: Nimbus Labs on Sat, 28 Nov  ›
```

- **The queue**, in this order, each a `ListRow` with the work in a sentence, its age or deadline, and one action: drives closing within 48 hours (the soonest, with a `Countdown`; Open), offers and outcomes to verify (oldest; Verify), students waiting for verification (oldest; Review), reports, appeals, questions and held posts to moderate (oldest report; Review), problem reports to answer (Answer), career paths, study uploads and the review queues (Review), students on the list who haven't joined (Invite), imports that failed this week (Open), the AI assistant needing attention (key invalid or 80% of the budget; Open), WhatsApp near its allowance (Open). Only what the member may act on; only what is above zero.
- **One tap**: the action opens the place the work is done (the drive, the offers queue, …). Nothing is done from Today blindly: inviting on WhatsApp still asks the placement cell to confirm the students opted in, per branch and batch.
- **All clear**: "Nothing waiting." and, for the placement cell, the next drive with its date.
- **API** (`GET /admin/overview`): each queue keeps its count and adds `since` (when the oldest item arrived) where the owning service has it (verification, offers and outcomes, reports, career paths); drives add the soonest closing drive and the next drive.
- The page title is **Today** (it was "Admin").

## U9b · One table pattern

- `DataTable`: compact rows, a sticky header, sort with `aria-sort`, selection with a sticky bulk bar, a row opening its side panel, columns that hide on phones.
- Verification requests: the table, bulk approve or reject from the bar, the request (ID photo, decide) in a side panel.
- Members: the table, the member's roles, capabilities and actions in a side panel.

## U9c (next)

Dashboards (analytics, readiness board, reports): four numbers at the top with the change against the previous period, then one chart per question; every number keeps `suppress()`; charts readable in dark mode and on a phone.

## States

- **Loading**: three row skeletons. **Error**: `ErrorState` with Retry. **All clear**: as above. **Offline**: the cached queue with the app's offline line.
- **Read-only staff** (faculty without the placement cell's permissions): only their own queues.

## Copy (en)

## Acceptance (U9a)

- A TPO opens Today and reaches the most urgent item's place in one tap; no row at zero; no title cut or wrapped one word per line at 360 in en, te or hi.
- Axe 0 on Today in both themes; targets ≥ 44 px.
