# U9 · Staff: Today, tables and the TPO console

Prompt 3, Part U, phase U9. Built on U1's primitives (`ListRow`, `ListSection`, `Countdown`, `Table`, `Toolbar`, `Sheet`) and U2's staff spaces (Today, Students, Placements, Content, College). Shipped in four parts: **U9a**, Today (the staff home); **U9b**, one table pattern for staff lists; **U9c**, dashboards that lead with four numbers and their change; **U9d**, the same tables on the recruiter portal and the platform console.

## Who it is for and the job

A placement officer (TPO), a faculty member or a college admin opening the app between classes: see what is waiting, oldest and most urgent first, and act on it in one tap. When nothing is waiting, know that, and what is next.

## Problems it fixes (§3)

| Problem                                                               | What U9 changes                                                                          |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A19: tiles whose titles wrap one word per line ("Not / joined / yet") | Rows: the work in a sentence that wraps as a sentence ("12 students haven't joined yet") |
| A19: a "Waiting" badge colliding with a truncated title ("Modera…")   | No badge: a row exists only when something is waiting                                    |
| A19: zero-count tiles at the same weight as urgent ones               | Items at zero are hidden; the order is by urgency (drives closing first)                 |
| The overview repeated every admin section under the tiles             | Gone: the staff spaces list their sections (U2)                                          |
| Members and activity shown as if they were work                       | One quiet line, "This week", linking to analytics                                        |

## U9a · Today (`features/admin/today.tsx`, `/admin`)

```
360 ──────────────────────────────
 Today
 ┌──────────────────────────────┐
 │ 1 drive closes in 2 days     │
 │ Nimbus Labs, in 5 hours      │
 │                     [Open]   │
 ├──────────────────────────────┤
 │ 3 offers to verify           │
 │ Oldest waiting 2 days        │
 │                    [Verify]  │
 ├──────────────────────────────┤
 │ 12 students haven't joined   │
 │                    [Invite]  │
 └──────────────────────────────┘
 This week: 1,204 members · 312 active   ›

All clear ────────────────────────
 Nothing waiting.
 Next drive: Nimbus Labs on Sat, 28 Nov  ›
```

- **The queue**, in this order, each a `ListRow` with the work in a sentence, its age or deadline, and one action: drives closing within 48 hours (the soonest, with a `Countdown`; Open), offers and outcomes to verify (oldest; Verify), students waiting for verification (oldest; Review), reports, appeals, questions and held posts to moderate (oldest report; Review), problem reports to answer (Answer), career paths, study uploads and the review queues (Review), students on the list who haven't joined (Invite), imports that failed this week (Open), the AI assistant needing attention (key invalid or 80% of the budget; Open), WhatsApp near its allowance (Open). Only what the member may act on; only what is above zero.
- **One tap**: the action opens the place the work is done (the drive, the offers queue, …). Nothing is done from Today blindly: inviting on WhatsApp still asks the placement cell to confirm the students opted in, per branch and batch.
- **All clear**: "Nothing waiting." and, for the placement cell, the next drive with its date.
- **API** (`GET /admin/overview`): each queue keeps its count and adds `since` (when the oldest item arrived) where the owning service has it (verification, offers and outcomes, reports, career paths); drives add the soonest closing drive and the next drive.
- The page title is **Today** (it was "Admin").

## U9b · One table pattern

- `DataTable`: compact rows, a sticky header, sort with `aria-sort`, selection with a sticky bulk bar, a row opening its side panel, columns that hide on phones.
- Verification requests: the table, bulk approve or reject from the bar, the request (ID photo, decide) in a side panel.
- Members: the table, the member's roles, capabilities and actions in a side panel.

## U9c · Dashboards: four numbers and their change

Analytics, Reports and the readiness board each open with four headline numbers. Under each, its change against the period before, in words with an arrow ("Up 12 on the 6 months before"), never colour alone. Then one chart or table per question, each titled with the question it answers. Every count keeps `suppress()`.

```

 [Last 6 months             ▾]
 [All branches ▾] [All batches ▾]
 ┌─────────────┬─────────────┐
 │ Mentorship  │ Students    │
 │ sessions    │ referred    │
 │ 48          │ 12          │

 │ the 6 months│ the 6 months│
 │ before      │ before      │
 ├─────────────┼─────────────┤
 │ Jobs posted │ Events held │
 │ 21          │ <5          │
 │ — Same as … │ Was 6 for … │
 └─────────────┴─────────────┘
 Are mentorship sessions happening every month?
 ▂▃▅▃▆▇  (table view one tap away)
 Are jobs being posted?
 ▃▃▂▅▄▆

```

| Screen          | The four numbers                                                                           | Compared with                                        |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Analytics       | Mentorship sessions, students referred, jobs posted, events held                           | The same length of time just before the period       |
| Reports         | Placement %, placed, median offer band, higher studies                                     | The batch before                                     |
| Readiness board | Students, ready (at or above the threshold), median score, need help before a drive closes | The board a week ago (kept as daily totals, 35 days) |

### Rules

- **A change only between two shown figures.** If either side is under 5, the change is not shown ("Was under 5 for …"), so a difference never reveals a small count (`kpiChange`, shared).
- **No earlier figure, said so.** A new college, or a board not yet a week old: "No earlier figure yet".
- **Which way is good** sets the colour only: fewer students needing help is good; the words say up or down either way.
- **Equal lengths of time.** "Last 6 months" runs from the first of the month five months back to today; the period before is the same number of days just before it, so a month in progress is not compared with a whole one.
- **The readiness board's week-ago figures** are the board's own daily totals (students, ready, median, needing help; no names, no scores per student), kept 35 days. A student's own snapshots (U7) are never read by the board.

### States

- Loading: four tile skeletons, then the charts. Error: `ErrorState` with Retry. Empty period: zeros, with the charts' empty lines.
- Dark and phone: the tiles are two across at 360; the change wraps under the number; the charts keep 12 px labels (`ColumnChart` lays out at its width) and the brand hue on the dark surface.

### Copy (en)

"Up {{n}} on {{against}}", "Down {{n}} on {{against}}", "Up {{n}} points on {{against}}", "Same as {{against}}", "Was {{n}} for {{against}}", "Was under 5 for {{against}}", "No earlier figure yet", "the {{count}} months before", "the {{batch}} batch", "last week".

### Acceptance (U9c)

- Each dashboard opens with four numbers, each with its change or the reason there is none; no change computed from a count under 5 (API tests).
- Axe 0 in both themes; nothing cut at 360 in en, te or hi; the charts' labels readable in dark mode.

## U9d · The recruiter portal and the platform console

The same table pattern (U9b) where Step 0.2 allowed it: the owner accepted ADR 0014 on 11 Nov 2026 (F1), so network and employer code may change.

| Screen                             | Columns                                                | A row opens                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Platform › Colleges                | College (monogram, slug), plan, members, status        | The college's panel: plan, members, Suspend or Resume (asks first)              |
| Platform › Support                 | Subject, college, updated, status                      | The report and its thread (unchanged)                                           |
| Platform › Employers               | Employer (domain), applied, status                     | The review (unchanged)                                                          |
| Platform › Traction                | College, then every measure, all sortable              | Nothing: aggregates only                                                        |
| Recruiter › Postings               | Posting (role, place, pay), closes, candidates, status | The posting: colleges and their decisions, Offer to colleges, Candidates, Close |
| Recruiter › a posting's candidates | Candidate (college, branch, batch), shared, stage      | The candidate: résumé, readiness, Shortlist or Not selected                     |

Headers stay `PageHeader` (title, icon, intro, the primary action), as on the staff screens. A "<5" in Traction sorts between 0 and 5, which says nothing more than "<5" does.

## States

- **Loading**: three row skeletons. **Error**: `ErrorState` with Retry. **All clear**: as above. **Offline**: the cached queue with the app's offline line.
- **Read-only staff** (faculty without the placement cell's permissions): only their own queues.

## Copy (en)

- "Today", "{{count}} drive closes in the next 2 days", "{{company}}, {{when}}", "{{count}} offers to verify", "Oldest waiting {{when}}", "{{count}} students waiting to be verified", "{{count}} reports and posts to check", "{{count}} problem reports to answer", "{{count}} career paths to review", "{{count}} students haven't joined yet", "Nothing waiting.", "Next drive: {{company}} on {{date}}", "This week".

## Acceptance (U9a)

- A TPO opens Today and reaches the most urgent item's place in one tap; no row at zero; no title cut or wrapped one word per line at 360 in en, te or hi.
- Axe 0 on Today in both themes; targets ≥ 44 px.
