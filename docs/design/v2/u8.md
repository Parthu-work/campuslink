# U8 · Ask: one assistant

Prompt 3, Part U, phase U8 (X8). Built on U1's primitives (`Segmented`, `Sheet`, `ListRow`, `Chip`) and U2's Learn space. Shipped in two parts: **U8a**, one Ask screen (the scope switch, one history, the career chat in the same layout as the study chat, questions in plain words, one entry point, the old addresses as redirects, "Ask about…" in search); **U8b**, the answer (citations that open source cards, refusals as a card with people to ask, copy, retry and feedback on every answer).

The servers stay as they are (X8): the study assistant is `ChatService` (`/study/chat`), the Career Copilot goes through `AiTasksService.stream` (`/copilot/chat`), each with its own rules, limits and budget. Only the way in, the chat screen and the history are shared.

## Who it is for and the job

A student with a question: about a subject ("explain the pumping lemma"), or about their career ("which drives can I register for?"). They should not have to know there are two assistants, or any command syntax.

- **Scope**: Study and Career, a `Segmented` switch, each shown only where the member has it (Study: the study feature and `study:read`; Career: placements and `copilot:use`). In the address: `/ask?scope=career&s=<conversation>`. Study keeps its subject picker and answer mode inside its panel.
- **History**: one list of both kinds of conversation, newest first, each marked Study or Career; opening one switches the scope. Beside the chat from 1024 px, in a sheet on phones. Delete one, or clear all (both kinds).
- **Career chat** (`features/copilot/career-panel.tsx`): the same frame as the study chat: a log that follows new text while the member is at the bottom, the composer docked with Send (and Stop while answering), Enter to send. Suggestions when the conversation is empty: "Which drives can I register for?", "Make me a plan for the next few weeks", "How can my résumé read better?", and "Help me prepare for Nimbus Labs" for the next drive the student can register for.
- **Plain words**: a suggestion sends the command it stands for; the member's message is shown as the words ("Quiz me on arrays", "Which drives can I register for?"), in the study chat too. Typed commands still work; the command menu appears only after typing "/".
- **Trust**: one line under the composer and **How answers work** (a sheet: where answers come from, that predictions are refused, that AI can be wrong and the sources are there to check). Questions left today, only under 5.
- **Ways in**: Ask in the Learn space (and You, where the Copilot used to be listed); the search palette offers **Ask about "…"** for what was typed, opening Ask with the question in the composer (not sent); the subject pages' Ask button is unchanged.

## U8b · The answer

- **Citations**: the numbered marker opens a source card (title, where it is from, the passage, Open it), in both chats.
- **Refusals**: a card with the reason and the people to ask (career: the human next steps; study: nearby topics and Ask your seniors in Q&A).
- **Career answers** get copy, ask again and thumbs (`POST /copilot/messages/:id/feedback`, own answers only).

## States

- **Loading**: the thread's skeleton; the history list's skeleton.
- **Empty**: the suggestions for the scope.
- **AI off or budget spent**: Study answers with passages only (as now); Career shows its sources-only answer (as now); the trust line says so.
- **Offline**: the composer says it needs a connection (the existing error); history from cache.
- **Minor**: Career needs `copilot:use`, which minors have only as the college allows; nothing new is shown to them.

## Acceptance (U8a)

- One Ask destination for a student; `/study/chat`, `/study/chat/:id` and `/copilot?c=` land on the same conversation in Ask.
- No `/command` shown anywhere a member did not type it; typed commands still work (e2e: `/help`, `/quiz`, the eligible drives).
- Axe 0 on Ask (both scopes) in both themes; the composer and Send are reachable by keyboard; targets ≥ 44 px.
