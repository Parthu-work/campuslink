# U11 · Accessibility and language

Prompt 3, Part U, phase U11. Nothing new on screen: every screen readable, reachable and operable by everyone, in English, Telugu and Hindi. Shipped in three parts: **U11a**, the audits' findings (axe in both themes, 44 px targets, meaning cut short in any language); **U11b**, keyboard paths and labels (every U0.2 journey with the keyboard alone, icon-only buttons, charts in words, the roadmap as an ordered list); **U11c**, motion and size (reduced motion respected, text at 200%).

## Who it is for and the job

A student using a screen reader or only a keyboard, a teacher with low vision who sets text large, a student reading in Telugu or Hindi on a 360 px phone: finish the same tasks as anyone else, without guessing what a cut-off label said.

## Rules

- **A cut title is a defect; a cut preview is not.** A message's first words or a post's opening may end in an ellipsis on purpose (the full text is one tap away). A heading, a name, a button's or a tab's label may not: it wraps, or the layout gives it the room.
- **Fix the cause, never the check.** A target under 44 px grows (or gains a hit area); a violation is fixed in the component, so every screen using it is fixed at once.
- **Keyboard order follows reading order.** No positive `tabindex`; a dialog traps focus and returns it to what opened it.

## Acceptance (U11)

- The capture audit's summary: 0 axe violations and 0 targets under 44 px; the language audit: no meaning cut short; the keyboard task run: every journey finished.
- Reduced motion and 200% text checks pass in e2e.
