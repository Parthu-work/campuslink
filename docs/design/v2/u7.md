# U7 · Career: placements, résumé, interviews, practice, readiness

Prompt 3, Part U, phase U7. Built on U1's primitives (`ActionCard`, `Countdown`, `Timeline`, `ScoreRing`, `ListRow`, `ListSection`, `Sheet`) and U2's Career space. Shipped in three parts: **U7a**, drives and readiness (Placements, a drive's page, Readiness, and "Drives that ask for these skills" on a career path); **U7b**, preparing (Practice as a short daily flow with "Practise this" from a path's step, Interviews, Companies with target companies asked on the first visit); **U7c**, the résumé.

## Who it is for and the job

A final-year student in placement season: see which drives they can register for and register before the deadline, know where they stand after each round, and see what will help most this week. Second and third years use the same space to prepare.

- **Header**: the title only (A6). The privacy line stays at the foot.
- **Record row**: CGPA and active backlogs in one line, where they come from ("From the college", "Checked by the placement cell", "Waiting for the placement cell"), **View** opens a sheet with every figure and "Something wrong? Ask the placement cell to correct it." No record: "Your marks aren't here yet" and View; the sheet says the placement cell adds them and, only where the record is not the college's, offers **Add your marks** (the existing self-report, checked by the placement cell). Nothing on the screen asks for a CGPA.
- **Drive card**: the company and the drive's title link to the drive (the whole card is the target; the button sits above it). Pay (`driveCtc`) and mode on one line. A `Countdown` while registration is open (warm within 48 hours). Then one status line: "You're eligible" / "The placement cell let you in" / the first reason in words (`reasonText`), "+2 more" when there are others / "Registered" with the current round / "Selected". **Register** on the card for an open, eligible drive: one tap when the drive has no questions and needs no résumé (toast "Registered for Nimbus Labs" with **Undo**, which withdraws); otherwise it opens the drive with the registration sheet open (`/placements/:id?register=1`).
- **Tabs** unchanged: For you, All drives, Registered, Offers.

## U7a · A drive (`features/placements/drive-page.tsx`)

- **Header** compact: company mark, company, title, then mode, venue and date in one line.
- **Registration** (beside on desktop, first on a phone after the header): as now, plus the `Countdown`; `?register=1` opens the sheet (or, for a one-tap drive, puts focus on Register).
- **Rounds** as a `Timeline`: each round's date (or "Round 2"), name and kind; the student's result as a badge once registered; the current round (the first without a final result, or the next by date) is marked.
- Home's Today **Register** goes to `?register=1`.

## U7a · Readiness (`features/progress/readiness.tsx`)

```
360 ─────────────────────────────────
 Readiness
     ╭───╮
    │ 48  │   [+6 this week]
     ╰───╯
 3 steps add 13 more
 Fix 2 résumé issues            +5 ›
 Do one mock interview          +4 ›
 Do a practice set              +4 ›
 What your score is made of
 Résumé                    14 of 20
 ████████████░░░░░
 Best ATS score: 72 of 100.
 Mock interviews            6 of 20
 …
 🔒 Only you and your placement cell …
```

- **Momentum** (new, `ReadinessView.momentum`): the change since the score the student last saw at least a week ago, "+6 this week" when that was within the last 8 days, "+6 since 3 Nov" when it was longer ago. Shown only when it went up; never a minus (no shame language). The first week has none.
  - **Stored**: one number a day per student (`ReadinessSnapshot`: day and score), written when the student opens Readiness, kept 35 days, in the data export, erased with the account. The placement cell's board never reads it. This changes "scores are never stored" in the readiness spec; recorded as a decision.
- **"3 steps add 13 more"**: the sum of the known gains of the top steps (steps with an unknown gain are listed but not added).
- **Parts** as rows in one list: the name, the points ("14 of 20"), a bar, the fact line, and that part's step only when it is not one of the top three.

## U7a · Drives on a career path (`features/placements/drives-on-path.tsx`)

- On a published path, for members who can see placements: open drives (registration open) with a role whose title is one of the path's outcomes (the same matching as "Seniors on this path": one title contains the other, ignoring case and the demo marker). Up to three rows: company, role, `Countdown`, "You're eligible" when true. Hidden when none.

## States

- **Loading**: skeletons in the shape of the drive cards, the record row, the ring and the rows.
- **Empty**: For you with no drives says you'll be told when one opens, with All drives as the action; Readiness with nothing to do says so.
- **Error**: `ErrorState` with Retry. **Offline**: the cached lists, with the app's offline line.
- **Minor**: placements are for final years; readiness is the student's own (never ranked); nothing new is shown to others.
- **Read-only** (staff viewing): no Register; the cards still show the countdown.

## Acceptance (U7a)

- Register for an eligible one-tap drive in **2 taps** from the Career tab (was 4); a drive with questions in 3 plus its fields.
- No title, company or role truncated at 360 in en, te or hi; axe 0 on Placements, a drive and Readiness in both themes; targets ≥ 44 px.
- Momentum is never shown without a stored score a week old, and never negative; snapshots are exported and erased (API tests).
- "Drives that ask for these skills" shows only drives whose role matches an outcome, and nothing otherwise.
