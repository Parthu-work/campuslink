Mentors are alumni from your own college who chose to help. A short, clear request is what gets a yes.

## Writing a good request

- Pick the session type: a quick chat, a video call, a mock interview or a résumé review.
- Say your goal in one or two sentences, your year and branch, and what you need from this session.
- Pick a time from the mentor's free slots when they offer them.

## What happens next

- The mentor accepts, offers another time, or declines with a short reply. You are told either way.
- A chat opens once they accept. Their contact details stay private.
- If there is no reply in two days, you are shown mentors who usually reply quickly. Sending to one of them withdraws the first request and does not count against your weekly requests.

## After the session

Rate the session; only the mentor and your college's admins see your feedback. You may also publish a short public review.
