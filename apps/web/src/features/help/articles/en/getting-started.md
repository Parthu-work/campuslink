Your college runs this app for its students, alumni and staff. Only verified members of your college can see what is inside.

## Signing in

- Sign in with your mobile number and a 6-digit code (on WhatsApp or by SMS), or with your email and password.
- College admins also use a code from an authenticator app.

## Your first steps

- **Verify your account** with your roll number, so your college knows you are a student or alumnus. Until then you can look around but not post or ask for mentorship.
- **Finish your profile**: a headline and a few skills help mentors say yes.
- **Find a mentor**: Alumni Match suggests alumni whose skills and goals fit yours, and says why.

## Finding your way

The tabs at the bottom (or the rail on a computer) hold what you use most. Everything else is in the menu behind your photo. Press Ctrl K (⌘K on a Mac) to search people, posts and jobs from anywhere.
