The app is built for slow and patchy connections.

## When you are offline

- Screens you opened recently stay readable: your profile, sessions, messages, job tracker, roadmaps, registered drives, study notes you opened and your feed. A note at the bottom says how old that copy is.
- An RSVP, saving a job and marking a roadmap step still work offline. They are sent when you are back online, and you are told when they are saved.
- Anything else, such as messages and posts, needs a connection.

## Shared phones

The saved copy is deleted when you sign out. Always sign out on a phone you share.

## Data saver

In Settings › Preferences, data saver loads smaller images, leaves stories out and loads videos only when you press play. On _Automatic_, it turns on by itself when your phone asks to save data or the connection is 2G.
