Your college decides what the app is used for, and the app is built to show as little about you as possible.

## Who sees what

- Your email and phone number are private unless you choose otherwise in your profile.
- Messages are seen only by the two people in the conversation; admins cannot read them.
- Your salary on an offer is seen only by you and the placement cell. Others see only bands and totals.
- If you are under 18, your contact details stay private, your feed is not ranked and you are not on leaderboards.

## Your choices

In **Settings › Privacy** you can change what your data is used for: mentor suggestions, email digests, WhatsApp updates and anonymous statistics.

## Your data

In **Settings › Your data** you can download everything the app holds about you, or delete your account. Deletion waits a short grace period, during which signing in cancels it.

## Questions or complaints

Your college's grievance officer is listed in Settings › Privacy and in the privacy policy.
