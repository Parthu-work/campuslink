The study assistant answers questions about your subjects from your college's own material: notes, slides, PDFs and video transcripts.

## Asking well

- Ask about a topic in your syllabus. Choose a subject at the top, or ask across all of them.
- Type / for commands: /videos and /notes find material on a topic, /summary and /explain explain it, /quiz and /flashcards help you revise.
- Ask in English, Telugu or Hindi; it answers in the language you write in.

## Its limits

- Answers come only from your college's material, with the page or moment they come from. If the material does not cover a topic, it says so instead of guessing.
- Answers are generated by AI and may contain mistakes. Check the sources it cites.
- When your college's monthly AI allowance is used up, you see the matching passages from the material instead.

## Privacy

Your conversations are private to you. Teachers see only counts, never your questions or your name.
