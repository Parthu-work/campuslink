The placement cell runs drives here: you see the ones you are eligible for, register in one tap and follow every round.

## Eligibility

- Each drive lists who can apply: branches, graduation year, CGPA, backlogs and 10th or 12th marks.
- Your marks come from the college's records. If you add your own, the placement cell verifies them before they count.
- If you are not eligible, the drive says why. The placement cell can check your record or let you in.

## Registering and rounds

- Register before registration closes; you get a reminder a day before.
- Some drives ask for a résumé or a few answers. You can withdraw while registration is open.
- Each round's result is shown to you as soon as the placement cell records it.

## Offers

Offers from campus drives appear in Placements › Offers by themselves. Report an off-campus offer with its letter, and the placement cell verifies it. Your exact salary is seen only by you and the placement cell.
