Your college keeps a list of its students and alumni. Verifying matches your account to your line on that list, so only real members of your college get in.

## How it works

- Enter your roll number and graduation year.
- If your mobile number or email is already on your college's record, you are verified at once. Otherwise a 6-digit code goes to the number or email on the record.
- If you joined from an invite link or a class code, you are verified already.

## If it does not work

- **"We couldn't match that roll number"**: check it against your ID card. Your college may not have added your batch yet.
- **"No email or number for this record"**: verify with a photo of your college ID instead. An admin checks it, and the photo is deleted 30 days after the decision.
- Still stuck? Use **Report a problem** on the Help page and choose _Verification_.

## Under 18?

A parent or guardian confirms with a code sent to the number on your admission record. Until then only verification and that step are open.
